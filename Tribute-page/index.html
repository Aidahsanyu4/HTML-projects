Dr.Norman Borlaug

Dr.Norman Borlaug, third from the left, trains biologists in Mexico on how to increase wheat yields- part of his life-long war on hunger.
Dr.Norman Borlaug was an American agroconomist who led initiatives worldwide that contributes to extensive increase in agriculture production. He was called father of the Green Revolution. He recieved the Nobel Peace in 1970, and is credited with saving over a billion people from starvation.
